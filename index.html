About Minseop Kim

Photographer, baseball fanatic, and most people call me Sup (Like what's up)

I find it magical how the camera becomes a bridge, connecting me to worlds I never imagined I could enter. While I photograph a range of subjects, I am especially drawn to sports. From baseball to skateboarding, my focus is to visualize the core elements and bring out the narrative in the scene, capturing meaningful moments and their unique cultural aspects. I photograph to tell stories. I’m driven to push the creative challenges and share my perspective through images.
